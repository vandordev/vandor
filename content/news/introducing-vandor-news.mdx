Vandor now has a dedicated news surface at `/news`.

This is where we want to publish the updates that belong close to the work itself:

- company announcements
- release notes
- product direction notes
- progress updates tied to the tools we maintain

`vx` documentation still belongs in `/vx/latest/docs`. That surface answers **how to use** the product. `Vandor News` is for everything that explains **what changed, why it matters, and where the work is going**.

## What belongs here

The bar is simple: if a piece is a direct update on what Vandor is shipping or changing, it belongs in `News`.

That includes:

- launch posts
- release narratives
- product direction notes
- company updates tied to the work

## What does not belong here

We do not want to turn `News` into a second docs tree or a general essay archive.

That means detailed CLI references, step-by-step setup instructions, versioned documentation, and broader technical essays stay elsewhere.

## Why this matters

A lot of teams only ship two kinds of pages:

- a polished marketing page
- a reference-heavy docs site

That leaves no room for straightforward updates that explain intent without turning into product docs.

`News` gives us a place to publish clear updates about the tools we maintain and the decisions behind them.
