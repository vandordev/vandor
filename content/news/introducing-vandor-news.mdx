Vandor now has a dedicated publication surface at `/news`.

This is where we want to publish the writing that sits between a homepage and a docs site:

- company announcements
- product essays
- release notes
- engineering notes that are meant to be read, not referenced

`vx` documentation still belongs in `/vx/latest/docs`. That surface answers **how to use** the product. `Vandor News` is for everything that explains **why we built it, what changed, and what we are learning**.

## What belongs here

The bar is simple: if a piece is useful as a narrative read, it belongs in `News`.

That includes:

- launch posts
- release narratives
- product direction notes
- ecosystem updates across Vandor

## What does not belong here

We do not want to turn `News` into a second docs tree.

That means detailed CLI references, step-by-step setup instructions, and versioned documentation stay in the product docs surface.

## Why this matters

A lot of teams only ship two kinds of pages:

- a polished marketing page
- a reference-heavy docs site

That leaves no room for writing that explains intent. We want Vandor to have that middle layer.

`News` gives us a place to write clearly about the products we maintain and the decisions behind them.
