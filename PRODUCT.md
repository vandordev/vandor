# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Vandor is for technical readers, collaborators, maintainers, and curious builders who want to understand what the organization is making and why it matters. Some arrive to explore the mission and ecosystem, some arrive for `vx`, and others arrive through shared writing or docs. They should feel that Vandor is a living public-interest technical organization, not just a product landing page.

## Product Purpose

Vandor is the public site for a mission-driven non-profit organization that builds tools, publishes writing, and maintains technical work in the open. `vx` is one of its products, and `/news` is one of its public surfaces, but the site as a whole should communicate that Vandor is an ecosystem with products, ideas, releases, and ongoing organizational life. Success looks like a homepage that makes the world feel alive and coherent, while still giving clear paths into products, docs, and writing.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, rigorous, elegant.

The tone should feel mission-driven, optimistic, calm, and technically literate. It should avoid loud startup polish, decorative theatrics, and product-marketing clichés. The emotional goal is trust through restraint and clarity, with enough visible life that the organization feels active, public, and in motion.

## Anti-references

- Substack clones
- Generic SaaS blogs
- Single-product startup landings
- Serif-heavy editorial magazine aesthetics
- Glassmorphism
- Over-polished startup marketing patterns

The site should not feel like a newsletter product, a single-product startup template, or a lifestyle magazine. It should also avoid decorative UI chrome that competes with reading or institutional seriousness.

## Design Principles

- Make Vandor read as an organization first, with products and writing as visible parts of a larger public ecosystem.
- Let the homepage feel alive through rhythm, sectioning, and evidence of ongoing work, not through noisy interface tricks.
- Make long-form reading feel primary, not secondary to navigation or product promotion.
- Separate editorial writing from product docs clearly, without making the editorial surface feel detached from Vandor.
- Use minimal chrome and deliberate hierarchy so the interface supports reading instead of announcing itself constantly.
- Express technical seriousness through rhythm, spacing, typography, and carefully directed imagery rather than dashboards, badges, or decorative complexity.
- Keep product references contextual and clear. Vandor is the frame, products like `vx` are part of the story, not the whole story.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and semantics throughout the reading experience. Motion should stay low-energy and non-distracting, with support for reduced-motion preferences. Typography should prioritize long-form readability on mobile and desktop, with stable line lengths, generous spacing, and no visual effects that reduce focus or legibility.
