# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Vandor News is for technical readers, collaborators, and curious builders who want to understand what Vandor is making and why it matters. They arrive in a reading mindset, usually from the landing page, product docs, or a shared article link, and they expect calm, credible long-form content rather than a noisy content feed.

## Product Purpose

Vandor is a brand and product ecosystem site built around Vandor as the organization and `vx` as one of its products. The `/news` surface exists to publish announcements, essays, release notes, and product writing that sit between a marketing landing page and product docs. Success looks like a publication experience that makes Vandor feel serious, thoughtful, and readable, while keeping docs and editorial clearly separated.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, rigorous, elegant.

The tone should feel precise, calm, and technically literate. It should avoid loud startup polish, decorative theatrics, and blog-template energy. The emotional goal is trust through restraint and clarity, with enough refinement that the interface feels designed rather than merely functional.

## Anti-references

- Substack clones
- Generic SaaS blogs
- Serif-heavy editorial magazine aesthetics
- Glassmorphism
- Over-polished startup marketing patterns

The site should not feel like a newsletter product, a startup content template, or a lifestyle magazine. It should also avoid decorative UI chrome that competes with reading.

## Design Principles

- Make long-form reading feel primary, not secondary to navigation or product promotion.
- Separate editorial writing from product docs clearly, without making the editorial surface feel detached from Vandor.
- Use minimal chrome and deliberate hierarchy so the interface supports reading instead of announcing itself constantly.
- Express technical seriousness through rhythm, spacing, and typography rather than dashboards, badges, or decorative complexity.
- Keep product references contextual and quiet. Vandor is the frame, products like `vx` are part of the story.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and semantics throughout the reading experience. Motion should stay low-energy and non-distracting, with support for reduced-motion preferences. Typography should prioritize long-form readability on mobile and desktop, with stable line lengths, generous spacing, and no visual effects that reduce focus or legibility.
